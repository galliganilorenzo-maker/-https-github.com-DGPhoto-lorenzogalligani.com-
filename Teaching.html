Lorenzo Galligani

Teaching & Sculpture

I am a sculptor and sculpture teacher based in Florence, Italy, with extensive experience in traditional techniques, contemporary sculptural practice and the teaching of students from different levels and backgrounds.

My teaching combines practical knowledge of materials and techniques with attention to artistic development, encouraging students to develop their own visual language, critical thinking and individual artistic direction.

Teaching Philosophy

Art is a fundamental language in every learner's educational journey: a powerful tool for inner exploration and connection with the world around us.

From the Greek wisdom of "Know thyself", to the Enlightenment motto "Dare to know", my educational aim is to guide students in using artistic expression as a key to critical, creative and personal understanding.
